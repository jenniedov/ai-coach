---
topic: "Customers and product"
tags: [customers, product, validation, feedback]
summary: "Talk to customers before building; product quality over marketing; test with people who have the problem."
---

1. [INFERRED] Validate a product idea by talking to people who actually have the problem, not friends who want to be nice.
2. [INFERRED] Product over everything: marketing accelerates a product that already works and exposes one that doesn't.
3. [INFERRED] Ask customers what they would pay today; a vague "yes, but" is a signal to change the offer.
