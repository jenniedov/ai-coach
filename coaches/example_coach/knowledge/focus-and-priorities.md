---
topic: "Focus and priorities"
tags: [focus, priorities, execution, launching]
summary: "Do one thing well before adding a second; execution beats ideas; decide fast and review with data."
---

1. [INFERRED] Running several new ventures at once dilutes attention and cash; pick the one with the strongest conviction and prove it first.
2. [INFERRED] An idea is not a business; execution is. Ship a small version quickly and reinvest what you learn.
3. [INFERRED] Make the decision, move on, and revisit it with real numbers rather than waiting for a perfect plan.
