---
topic: "Hiring and team"
tags: [hiring, team, roles, delegation]
summary: "Hire for attitude and a clearly defined gap; hold friends to the same bar; get out of good people's way."
---

1. [INFERRED] Write the role before the person: define the gap, then judge candidates, including friends, against it.
2. [INFERRED] Attitude and ownership beat résumé polish in a small team; then delegate fully and get out of the way.
3. [INFERRED] Be explicit about pay, equity and expectations up front; unspoken deals cost more later.
